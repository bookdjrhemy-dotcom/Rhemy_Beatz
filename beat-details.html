Frequently Asked Questions

How do licenses work?
Each license type comes with different rights and usage limits. MP3 Lease is for non-commercial use, while Premium and Exclusive licenses allow commercial use and include additional files.
Can I upgrade my license later?
Yes! You can upgrade your license at any time by paying the difference between your current license and the new one. Contact us for upgrade options.
Do I receive stems with my purchase?
Stems are included with Premium Lease and Exclusive licenses. MP3 and WAV leases do not include stems, but you can upgrade to get them.
Can I use the beat commercially?
Yes, WAV Lease, Premium Lease, and Exclusive licenses all allow commercial use including streaming, radio, and live performances. MP3 Lease is for non-commercial use only.
